# No em dashes

Do not use em dashes (or spaced en dashes standing in for them) in anything
written for Andy: chat responses, code comments, commit messages and PR
descriptions alike.

**Why:** Andy finds them distracting, and they read as a tell in generated text.

**How to apply:** Break the sentence instead. A comma, a colon, parentheses or
a full stop and a second sentence covers every case an em dash would. Hyphens
in compound words ("built-in", "read-only") and ranges ("lines 10-20") are
fine.
